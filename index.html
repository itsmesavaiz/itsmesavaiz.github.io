02 · MS Research

Teaching computers Pakistan Sign Language.

In progress · MS thesis · 2025 to present

Pakistan Sign Language alphabet recognition with a hybrid CNN + MediaPipe model

Most sign language tools are built for American Sign Language. Deaf people in Pakistan use their own language, with their own Urdu alphabet signs, and very few tools support it. My thesis builds a model that recognises PSL alphabet signs from a normal camera. It combines a convolutional neural network (CNN), which looks at the hand image, with MediaPipe hand landmarks, which track 21 points on the hand. I am training and testing it on the UAlpha40 dataset of Urdu alphabet signs.

Step 1 · Done

Literature & data

Reviewed sign language recognition work and selected the UAlpha40 dataset.

Step 2 · Now

Hybrid model

Combining CNN image features with MediaPipe hand landmarks.

Step 3 · Next

Training & testing

Measuring accuracy on unseen signers and real camera input.

Step 4 · Next

Thesis write-up

Reporting results and comparing with existing PSL methods.

03 · Project

Sign Flow: fingerspelling to text.

BS Final Year Project · CUSIT · Supervisor: Dr. Ikram Ullah

ASL Fingerspelling Recognition App

An Android app that turns hand signs into text.

About 70 million people in the world are deaf, and most hearing people do not know sign language. Sign Flow uses the phone camera to read American Sign Language fingerspelling, one letter at a time, and shows it as text. It also works the other way: a hearing person types or speaks, and the app shows the matching signs.

MediaPipe finds 21 joint points on the hand in each camera frame. A CNN model then classifies the hand shape as a letter. There is also a learning mode with an ASL dictionary, practice activities and a learning score.

Android · Java + XMLPythonCNNMediaPipe HandsSQLiteReal-time recognition
  • Recognise fingerspelled letters in real time from a phone camera.
  • Translate letters into text, and text or voice back into signs.
  • Help ASL learners practise and track progress with a score.
  • Give interpreters and teachers a quick support tool.
